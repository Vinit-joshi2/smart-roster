How the roster is built

Mandatory vs soft

Mandatory (shown as blocking issues): every process must have at least its minimum staff on every operating day; nobody may be rostered while on approved leave/comp-off; nobody may serve more brands in a day than their limit (double-booking). Soft (warnings/info): preferred weekly-off day, max {{ MAX_CONSEC }} consecutive working days, one weekly off per week. When they conflict, coverage always wins and the soft rule is reported.

A brand's calendar is separate from an employee's schedule: a 365-day brand is open every day, but each person still gets weekly offs, so the generator must bridge the gap with other staff or backups.

Assumptions

  • One employee can serve all processes of a brand on the same day. Load is counted in distinct brands per day (default limit 3).
  • Weekly rest is per Monday–Sunday week (1 off by default; configurable per process or person). Leave and comp-off days count as rest for that week; public holidays do not replace the weekly off.
  • Partial weeks at month boundaries shorter than 4 days get no quota and are ignored by the consecutive-days check.
  • On a public holiday, people with no operating process are marked H. Someone working a holiday earns a comp-off (shown in the employee view) – the app does not auto-schedule it.
  • Backups are first choice for cross-cover; if none is free the engine ranks anyone else (same brand → same pod → lowest load). Cover shifts are explicit and visible (+ marker).
  • Holidays in the sample data are illustrative.

Engine in one paragraph

1) Mark fixed unavailability (leave, comp-off, holidays). 2) For each week place weekly offs, most-constrained people first, choosing the day with the lowest cost (coverage deficit ≫ preference ≫ spread/consecutive days). 3) Repair pass moves offs away from gap days. 4) Fill what is still short with cross-cover. 5) Run the same validator used for manual edits and explain anything unresolved.
